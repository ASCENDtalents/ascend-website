KNOW YOUR LEADERSHIP STYLE

What Type of AI Leader Are You?

The AI age is here. The question isn't whether you'll deal with AI — it's how you'll navigate it based on who you actually are. Recognize yourself below.

🚀
Enthusiastic Pioneer
The Enthusiastic Pioneer

You're excited about AI and eager to adopt it. You test tools quickly, share discoveries, and love being at the forefront of change.

What you need Strategy to channel your enthusiasm into lasting impact — not just novelty-chasing.
😰
Overwhelmed Observer
The Overwhelmed Observer

AI feels like a tidal wave. You're watching it happen but unsure where to start. Fear of making the wrong move is keeping you still.

What you need A safe, structured starting point that turns overwhelm into confident first steps.
🤔
Skeptical Realist
The Skeptical Realist

You've seen tech trends come and go. You need proof before buy-in. Your critical eye is valuable — if channeled into smart decisions.

What you need Evidence-based frameworks that respect your judgment and build genuine conviction.
♟️
Strategic Planner
The Strategic Planner

You think in systems. You're mapping AI's impact on your org methodically — but execution is getting ahead of your roadmap.

What you need Decision-making tools to align AI adoption with your leadership vision — and bring your team along.
🔭
Curious Explorer
The Curious Explorer

You ask great questions about AI but haven't committed to a direction yet. You're gathering intel, building mental models, waiting for clarity.

What you need Guided exploration that turns curiosity into applied skills and real-world confidence.
⚙️
Practical Adopter
The Practical Adopter

You want AI to work for you — not theories, not trends. You're focused on tasks, efficiency, and solving real problems today.

What you need Hands-on, workflow-first training that plugs AI directly into how you already work.

Whichever leader you are, ASCEND has a program built for your starting point.
