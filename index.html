Meet the Founder

"I know what it feels like to be left behind — and I refused to let that happen to anyone else."

I've been there. Applying to BPO companies when the industry was already shaking. Watching talented people get passed over — not because they lacked ability, but because the world moved and nobody showed them how to move with it.

Now AI is doing the same thing — but at a much bigger scale and a much faster speed. Yes, AI is displacing jobs. But it is also creating an enormous number of new ones — for the people who are ready.

I built ASCEND because readiness shouldn't be a privilege. Whether you're a barangay leader trying to serve your community better, or a job seeker who just needs someone to show them where to start — you deserve a seat at this table.

Let's make it happen. Together.
